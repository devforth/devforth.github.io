What AdminForth gives you

Your agents can already do the work. This is what keeps it safe.

01 · Security

Your data stays yours, and stays patched.

Your records live in your own database on your server. Agents get in through one locked door: sign-in, a separate key per agent that you can revoke, two-factor and passkeys for people.

The parts you can’t review yourself come from AdminForth, one framework our team maintains. Security fixes ship with its updates. Updating is a version bump your agent runs; skills tell it how.

02 · Your team and their agents

Everyone works inside your rules, and you see who did what.

Your team uses agents too, on the same records. Each person, and each of their agents, sees and changes only what their role allows. The server checks every request, from a browser or from an agent.

The audit log names the person and the agent for every change. When something looks wrong, you know who, when, through what, and what it was before.

How it works

One operation, through the agent.

Northstar Services, a made-up maintenance company, runs on an admin built with AdminForth. Maya owns it; Tom and Priya are managers. Each of them talks to their own agent.

“Which follow-ups are overdue?” then “Set the next action for REQ-1042.” The agent reads and writes through the app’s MCP tools, as Maya, with her permissions.

Do I need a developer?

No. You describe the work, your agent writes the code on AdminForth, and you check the result in the browser. You still decide when a change is ready for your team. Hosting and backups are set up with the agent, and you own them.

Is this a ready-made CRM?

No. AdminForth is an open-source framework for building your own business admin: customers, orders, jobs, stock, invoices, whatever you run. Your agent builds the parts you need, around your fields and your rules.

Does an AI provider see my data?

Your app and database run where you host them. Whatever your agent reads through MCP goes to that agent’s model provider, under the permissions of the person who connected it. Compatible self-hosted models work too.

What about updates and security fixes?

The security parts, sign-in, sessions, two-factor, permissions, MCP, audit, live in the AdminForth framework and its plugins, which our team maintains. Your app updates by bumping their versions, which your agent can run. Your own code stays small: your fields and rules.

Can I change the admin later?

Yes. Ask your agent for the next field, stage, part or report. Every generated app carries instructions and skills for coding agents, so each change builds on what is there. Check changes before your team relies on them.

Where will the app run?

The first version runs on your computer. For your team it goes on a server you control; your agent prepares the deployment, you decide where it runs and how it is backed up.
